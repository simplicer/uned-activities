```markdown
## 🎯 What is UNED Activities Finder?

A free, multilingual platform that helps you discover, search, and stay informed 
about UNED (Universidad Nacional de Educación a Distancia) extension courses and activities.

### Why You Need It

UNED offers hundreds of extension activities each year, but they're scattered across 
multiple pages and hard to find. This platform solves that by:

- Centralizing all UNED activities in one searchable place
- Supporting 6 languages (Spanish, English, Catalan, Valencian, Basque, Galician)
- Letting you filter by price, topic, schedule, and location
- Notifying you about new activities matching your interests

### Who Should Use It?

- Students looking for extension courses
- Professionals seeking continuous education
- Anyone interested in UNED learning opportunities
```

#### Section 5: How to Use
```markdown
## 📖 How to Use

### Browsing Activities

1. Go to the Activities tab
2. Browse the latest activities or use search
3. Click any activity card for full details
4. Click the link to register on UNED's official website

### Searching with Filters

1. Use the search box for keywords (e.g., "Python", "Design")
2. Apply filters on the left side:
   - **Price:** From-to range
   - **Language:** Your preferred language
   - **Format:** Online, In-person, Hybrid
   - **Duration:** Short courses, Workshops, Full programs
   - **Location:** If in-person
3. Results update instantly

### Saving Searches (Login Required)

1. Create a search with your favorite filters
2. Click the "Save Search" button
3. Give it a name (e.g., "Python Courses")
4. Access saved searches anytime from your account

### Getting Notifications

1. Sign up or log in to your account
2. Go to Settings → Notifications
3. Choose when you want to hear about new activities:
   - **Immediate:** Instant notifications (can be a lot)
   - **Daily Digest:** Once per day
   - **Weekly Digest:** Summaries each week
4. Select topics you care about (optional)
5. Save preferences

### Understanding Activity Details

When you click an activity, you'll see:
- **Activity Name & Description:** Full details from UNED
- **Dates & Time:** When the activity runs
- **Prices:** All costs for different participant types
- **Instructor:** Who will teach it
- **Format:** Online, in-person, or hybrid
- **Language:** What language it's in
- **Enrollment Link:** Direct link to UNED registration
```
